Facing deportation in Germany – what matters now

Act immediately. Deadlines are short, and after the departure deadline the date of deportation is not announced (§ 59(1) Residence Act). Go to a free counselling centre today.

Deadlines at a glance

Voluntary departureusually 7–30 days (§ 59)
Objection/lawsuit (Ausländerbehörde)usually 1 month – see Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung
Lawsuit against BAMF asylum decision2 weeks, in certain cases 1 week (§ 74 AsylG)
Medical certificatewithout delay (§ 60a(2c))

Step by step

  1. Read the letter carefully

    Find the departure deadline (normally 7 to 30 days) and the “Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung” with the deadline for an objection or lawsuit. Note the date you received it.

  2. Get advice today

    Free help: migration counselling centres, refugee councils and legal advice services – links below. On a low income, the local court (Amtsgericht) gives you a Beratungshilfeschein for a lawyer.

  3. Asylum decision from BAMF?

    Very short deadlines apply: lawsuit usually within 2 weeks, in certain cases 1 week (§ 74 Asylum Act). Do not wait a single day.

  4. Request to see your file

    You or your adviser may see your file. Template below.

  5. Ill? Get a medical certificate at once

    An illness is only considered if you submit a qualified medical certificate without delay. It should contain: diagnosis, severity, ICD-10 code, consequences without treatment, and medicines with their active ingredient (§ 60a(2c)).

  6. Deportation impossible? Apply for a Duldung

    If deportation is impossible for factual or legal reasons – e.g. no passport – it must be suspended (Duldung, § 60a(2)). A Duldung can also be granted for urgent humanitarian or personal reasons.

  7. More time to leave

    The departure deadline can be extended in individual cases (§ 59(1)), e.g. for children's school or giving up a flat. Template below.

  8. Hardship commission

    Every federal state has a hardship commission (§ 23a). It decides itself whether to take a case. Usually excluded: serious crimes, or if a concrete deportation date is already set. Best go through a counselling centre.

  9. Entry ban

    After deportation an entry ban usually applies (§ 11). It can be shortened or lifted, e.g. for personal reasons worthy of protection.

  10. Keep key papers ready

    Copies of passport, decisions, work contract, school and medical documents. Give the phone number of your adviser or lawyer to a trusted person.

Always write letters to German authorities in German. Replace the yellow fields with your details. Keep a copy and proof of sending (registered mail, fax report or email).
