Residence permit or visa expiring in Germany – what to do?

Most important: apply for the extension before your permit expires – even if you cannot get an appointment. Your permit then stays valid until the authority decides.

Deadlines at a glance

Extension applicationbefore your permit expires
Reply to hearing letterthe deadline in the letter
Objection or lawsuitusually 1 month from delivery (see Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung)
Instruction missing or wrongusually 1 year (§ 58(2) VwGO)

Step by step

  1. Apply before the expiry date

    If you apply before your permit expires, it continues to be valid until the decision (§ 81(4) Residence Act). This does not apply to a Schengen visa.

  2. No appointment? Apply in writing

    Do not wait for an appointment. Send the application in writing, by online form, fax or email before expiry, and keep the proof. Template below: “Application for extension”.

  3. Ask for a Fiktionsbescheinigung

    The authority must give you a certificate showing the effect of your application (§ 81(5)). It proves to employers, the Jobcenter and landlords that your stay is still allowed. Your old permit continues with its conditions – including permission to work if it was in it.

  4. Already expired?

    Apply immediately. For a late application the authority may order that your permit continues to avoid unreasonable hardship – but it does not have to. Go to a counselling centre at the same time.

  5. Ask before travelling abroad

    Before any trip, ask your Ausländerbehörde whether you can re-enter with your certificate.

  6. Got a hearing letter (Anhörung)?

    This usually means the authority intends to refuse. Reply within the deadline with your reasons and documents. Template below.

  7. Got a refusal?

    Read the “Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung” at the end of the decision. It says whether you must file an objection (Widerspruch) with the authority or a lawsuit (Klage) at the administrative court – this differs between federal states – and the deadline (usually 1 month). If it is missing or wrong, usually 1 year applies (§ 58 VwGO).

  8. Warning: an objection does not protect you automatically

    An objection or lawsuit against the refusal of a residence permit has no suspensive effect (§ 84(1) Residence Act). To be allowed to stay until the decision, an urgent application to the administrative court is usually needed. Get help immediately.

  9. Filing a lawsuit without a lawyer

    You can file a lawsuit in writing or have it recorded at the administrative court office (§ 81 VwGO). On a low income you can get Beratungshilfe (apply at the local court, Amtsgericht) or legal aid.
