Grundsicherungsgeld (formerly Bürgergeld): cuts, hearing, objection

Important: since 1 July 2026, Bürgergeld is called 'Grundsicherungsgeld'. Your existing decisions remain valid; letters may still say 'Bürgergeld' for a while. If you receive a hearing letter (Anhörung), answer quickly in writing and attach proof of an important reason.

Step by step

  1. What has changed?

    On 1 July 2026 Bürgergeld was replaced by Grundsicherungsgeld. Existing decisions remain valid. Forms, decisions and letters are being updated step by step – they may still say 'Bürgergeld'. The duties to cooperate have become stricter.

  2. What are your duties?

    It is a breach of duty if, despite a written warning, you do not prove the job-search efforts required or you refuse or quit reasonable work, training or a measure – including an integration course or job-related German course (§ 31 SGB II). This does not apply if you explain and prove an important reason.

  3. What happens after a breach?

    Grundsicherungsgeld is cut by 30% of the standard rate for three months (§§ 31a, 31b SGB II). If you meet your duties or seriously declare you are willing to, the cut is lifted – after one month at the earliest. Payments for rent and heating are not cut. In cases of exceptional hardship there is no cut.

  4. Jobcenter appointments

    If you repeatedly miss appointments without an important reason, the benefit is cut by 30% of the standard rate for one month each time (§ 32 SGB II). Cancel appointments you cannot attend in advance and send proof, e.g. a doctor's note.

  5. Careful: refusing reasonable work

    If you refuse reasonable work that is available immediately, your entitlement to the standard rate can be removed completely; rent and heating are then paid to the landlord (§ 31a(7) SGB II). Get help from a counselling service immediately.

  6. The hearing – your chance

    Before the Jobcenter decides on a cut, it must hear you (§ 24 SGB X). You can ask to be heard in person (§ 31a(2) SGB II). Reply on time, explain your important reason and attach proof. Template below.

  7. Objection and free help

    You can object to a decision on a cut within one month (§ 84 SGG). Free help is available from social counselling (Caritas, Diakonie, AWO), migration counselling and with a legal-aid certificate (Beratungshilfe) from the local court.
