Integration course: admission, costs and test – step by step

Important: since 2026, voluntary participants only get a place to a limited extent. Apply for the fee exemption and the travel subsidy before the course starts – they are usually not granted retroactively. If you complete the course successfully, you can get half of the fee back.

Deadlines at a glance

Admission by the BAMFvalid for 1 year (§ 5(3) IntV)
Exemption from the course feeapply before the course starts – not retroactive
Travel cost subsidyapply before the course section starts (§ 4a IntV)
Course fee (Kostenbeitrag)usually €2.29 per lesson, due at the start of each course section
Course length700 lessons: language course + orientation course (§ 10 IntV)
50% of the fee backif successful within 2 years of your first entitlement (§ 9(6) IntV)
Objection to refused admission1 month after receiving the decision – see the legal instructions (§ 70 VwGO)

Step by step

  1. Who can take part?

    If the immigration office or the Jobcenter obliged you to attend, or you have a right to it (e.g. with a new residence permit), you get a certificate of entitlement (Teilnahmeberechtigung). Everyone else must apply to the BAMF for admission (§ 44(4) AufenthG) – since 2026 only if places are free. Priority: people with a residence permit under § 24 AufenthG (Ukraine) and Germans and EU citizens whose German is not enough for work or training, plus their families (§ 5(4) IntV).

  2. Apply to the BAMF for admission

    Submit a complete application with proof. You can also apply through a course provider and apply for the fee exemption at the same time (§ 5(1) IntV). Admission is valid for one year. If you are refused, the decision explains how to object.

  3. Course fee – and how to be exempted

    The fee is half of the cost reimbursement rate (§ 9(1) IntV), usually €2.29 per lesson. On application, people receiving Grundsicherungsgeld (SGB II), social assistance, AsylbLG benefits or unemployment benefit, and low-income employees are exempt (§ 9(2) IntV). If the Jobcenter obliged you to attend, you are usually exempt automatically. If your situation changes, tell the BAMF immediately.

  4. Travel cost subsidy

    If you are exempt because of Grundsicherungsgeld, social assistance, AsylbLG benefits or unemployment benefit, you can apply for a flat-rate travel subsidy – before the course section starts (§ 4a IntV). It is usually granted if the course is more than 3 km from your home.

  5. Attend regularly – ask for a certificate

    On request, the course provider must give you a certificate of regular attendance, even before the course ends (§ 14(6) IntV). The immigration office and the Jobcenter can ask for it. Excuse absences in writing. You pay the fee for the whole course section, even if you miss classes (§ 9(4) IntV).

  6. Changing the course provider

    A change is possible for special reasons, e.g. moving house, switching between part-time and full-time, childcare, training or work (§ 14(4) IntV). Talk to your course provider first.

  7. Final tests and certificate

    The course ends with the 'Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer' (A2–B1) and the 'Leben in Deutschland' test (§ 17 IntV). The BAMF pays for the tests once; if you fail the language test before your hours run out, it also pays for a second try. With B1 and a passed orientation test you get the 'Zertifikat Integrationskurs'. You can repeat course sections at your own cost (§ 14(5) IntV).

  8. If successful: 50% back

    If you complete the course successfully within 2 years of your first entitlement, the BAMF can refund half of the fee you paid (§ 9(6) IntV). Template below.
