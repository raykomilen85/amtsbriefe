Applying for social benefits in Germany – without losing money

Most important tip: apply immediately – a short informal letter is enough. Housing benefit, child supplement and basic income support are only paid from the month the application arrives. Forms and documents can follow later.

Deadlines at a glance

Housing benefitfrom the month of application – not retroactive
Child supplementfrom the month of application, 6 months each
Parental allowanceretroactive max. 3 months of life
Maintenance advanceretroactive max. 1 month
Basic income (SGB XII)from the 1st of the application month
Action for inactionsocial court 6 months (objection 3), administrative court usually 3 months

Step by step

  1. Housing benefit (Wohngeld)

    Responsible: the housing benefit office of your town. Paid from the first day of the month you apply (§ 25(2) WoGG). People receiving Grundsicherungsgeld (formerly Bürgergeld/citizen's income) or basic income support that covers housing costs do not get housing benefit on top (§ 7 WoGG).

  2. Child supplement (Kinderzuschlag)

    For parents with a low income who receive child benefit. Responsible: Familienkasse. Paid from the month of application, granted for 6 months at a time – then apply again (§ 6a BKGG).

  3. Parental allowance (Elterngeld)

    Responsible: the Elterngeld office of your state or district. Retroactively only for the last 3 months of the child's life before the month the application arrives (§ 7 BEEG). So apply no later than the child's 3rd month.

  4. Maintenance advance (Unterhaltsvorschuss)

    If the other parent does not pay child maintenance. Responsible: the youth welfare office (Jugendamt). Retroactively for at most the month before the application – and only if you made reasonable efforts to get maintenance (§ 4 UVG).

  5. Basic income in old age / reduced earning capacity

    Responsible: the social welfare office (Sozialamt). The application takes effect from the first day of the month it is made (§ 44(2) SGB XII). Condition: you already meet the requirements for basic income in that month.

  6. Pension and insurance record

    Responsible: Deutsche Rentenversicherung. Have your record checked early – periods abroad can count too. Free service line: 0800 1000 4800.

  7. No answer from the office?

    Send a status inquiry (template below). Without a decision you can sue for inaction: at the social court after 6 months, after an objection after 3 months (§ 88 SGG); at the administrative court usually after 3 months (§ 75 VwGO). Elterngeld disputes go to the social court (§ 13 BEEG); otherwise your decision letter names the court.

  8. Find the responsible office

    Call the authority number 115 or use the Familienportal search (links below). Keep a copy and proof of sending of every application.
