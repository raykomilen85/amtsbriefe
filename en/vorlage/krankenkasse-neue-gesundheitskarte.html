Sample letter: New health insurance card

The letter is in German – this is the text you send to the office. The explanations are in English.

[Vor- und Nachname]
[Straße und Hausnummer]
[PLZ und Ort]

Krankenkasse
[Anschrift der Behörde]

[Ort], [Datum]

Versichertennummer: [Versichertennummer]

Betreff: Neue elektronische Gesundheitskarte

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

meine elektronische Gesundheitskarte muss ersetzt werden. Grund: [Grund (z. B. verloren, defekt, Name geändert)].

Bitte senden Sie mir eine neue Karte zu. Falls Sie ein aktuelles Foto benötigen, teilen Sie mir das bitte mit.

Mit freundlichen Grüßen

[Unterschrift]
[Vor- und Nachname]

What you need

  • Full name (Vor- und Nachname)
  • Street and house number (Straße und Hausnummer)
  • Postcode and town (PLZ und Ort)
  • Reason (e.g. verloren, defekt, Name geändert) (Grund (z. B. verloren, defekt, Name geändert))
  • Versichertennummer

What to do

  1. Open the online template and enter your details – the gaps are filled automatically.
  2. Print the letter or send it by email, and sign it.
  3. Keep proof: a receipt stamp on your copy, a fax transmission report or registered mail.
  4. Watch the deadline – the deadline calculator helps.

Good to know

  • Report a lost or stolen card immediately so nobody can misuse it.
  • Until the new card arrives: the doctor's practice can check your insurance with the insurer, or the insurer sends a substitute certificate.
  • From age 15 the card needs a photo. You can often upload it in the app.
  • Wrong details (name, gender) must be reported with proof, e.g. ID or marriage certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Does a new health card cost anything?

Usually not. If you lose it repeatedly, the insurer may charge a fee.

How do I send the letter to Krankenkasse?

Sign it and keep a copy. Safest: hand it in personally and have your copy stamped, send it by fax with a transmission report, or by registered mail (Einschreiben).

Is the template free?

Yes. All templates on amtsbriefe.de are free and need no sign-up. What you enter stays in your browser.

Is there an explanation in my language?

Yes. The explanations are available in 13 languages. The letter itself is always in German, because that is how you send it to the office.
